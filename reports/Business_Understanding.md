# Business Understanding

---

# Company Background

The company operates in the retail industry and manages inventory across multiple product categories. Efficient inventory planning is essential to ensure product availability while minimizing inventory costs.

---

# Business Problem

Demand uncertainty makes inventory planning challenging. Inaccurate demand estimation often results in stockouts, excess inventory, reduced customer satisfaction, and increased operational costs.

The organization requires a forecasting system that supports better inventory planning through data-driven insights.

---

# Current Challenges

- Inaccurate demand forecasting
- Frequent stockouts
- Excess inventory
- Seasonal demand fluctuations
- Limited inventory visibility
- Inefficient inventory planning

---

# Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Forecast future product demand.
- Improve inventory planning.
- Reduce stockout risk.
- Reduce overstock risk.
- Analyze product performance.
- Support business decisions through dashboards.

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The project will evaluate the following business metrics:

- Total Sales
- Forecast Accuracy
- Stockout Rate
- Overstock Rate
- Inventory Turnover
- Revenue Trend
- Category Performance

---

# Expected Business Impact

The proposed solution is expected to:

- Improve demand forecasting accuracy.
- Reduce inventory holding costs.
- Minimize stock shortages.
- Improve inventory utilization.
- Support faster and better business decisions.

---

# Project Scope

The project includes:

- Data preprocessing
- Exploratory data analysis
- Feature engineering
- Demand forecasting
- Inventory risk analysis
- Dashboard development
- Executive reporting

---

# Assumptions

- Historical sales data is available and reliable.
- Inventory data accurately represents stock levels.
- Calendar information captures relevant seasonal patterns.

---

# Constraints

- Forecast quality depends on data quality.
- Unexpected market events are not explicitly modeled.
- Forecast accuracy may vary across products and seasons.
